Figure 4. Topological organization and within-cell-type expression heterogeneity recovery across immune populations (GSE120575). (A–E) UMAP embeddings depicting the topological organization of immune cell types for the (A) observed post-treatment reference, alongside reconstructed cell proxies generated by (B) scGen, (C) WOT, (D) CellRank, and (E) Leca-VC. (F) Quantitative comparison of post-treatment gene expression fidelity across the evaluated methods. (G–I) Evaluation of fine-grained transcriptomic heterogeneity recovery, showing (G) a heatmap of the dispersion ratio (log2 predicted vs. observed standard deviation) across specific immune populations, (H) summary dispersion error, and (I) overall distribution distance for scGen, CellRank, and Leca-VC.
