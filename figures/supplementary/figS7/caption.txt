Fig. S7. Traceable cellular program updates and PhysiCell writeback in GSE267904. Representative consecutive decisions from the frozen de-identified GSE267904 simulation are shown. (A) A fibroblast/myofibroblast Agent retained a matrix-remodeling program while its TGF-β-like and matrix-remodeling outputs increased from 0.12 to 0.32 and from 0.18 to 0.38, respectively. This represents increased activation of a matrix-remodeling program rather than a change in annotated cell identity. (B) A macrophage Agent changed from inflammatory recruitment to a resolution-transition program, with inflammatory output decreasing from 0.42 to 0.12 and resolution output increasing from 0.08 to 0.42. (C) An alveolar epithelial Agent changed from homeostatic maintenance to stress-adaptive repair, accompanied by increases in damage-associated output from 0.05 to 0.12 and resolution output from 0.06 to 0.22. (D) Across 665 communication values emitted during 95 K = 100 Agent-interval decisions, validated Agent outputs were written unchanged to the PhysiCell executor (maximum absolute difference, 0). These examples demonstrate that simulated program updates can be traced to explicit, schema-constrained Agent outputs and their executor-level implementation; they should not be interpreted as independently validated cell-fate conversions.
