Fig. S6. Mechanistic transparency of LLM-driven single-cell policy generation in the Leca-VC system. At simulation step 60, a representative myofibroblast agent (GSM8281384 V10A20-072-A1 agent 027) evaluates its local microenvironmental constraints to compute an updated biological program. (Left) Input Context: The LLM decision engine receives real-time, normalized spatial signals showing exposure to a highly pro-fibrotic environment, driven by elevated TGF-β (0.57), inflammatory signals (0.53), and tissue injury (0.46). Concurrently, the agent’s internal state registers an accumulating fibrosis memory (0.10), indicating the onset of pathological locking. (Right) Output Program: Rather than relying on latent-space interpolations, the LLM translates the specific input state into a mechanically coherent biological response. The generated policy assigns a high extracellular matrix (ECM) deposition output (0.90) and a high survival output (0.80), while assigning a low homeostatic repair output (0.10). The sustained memory gain action (0.70) establishes a positive feedback loop, driving the cell further into an irreversible fibrotic state.
