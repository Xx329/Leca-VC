GSE120575 cell-type composition benchmark. Broad cell-type proportions are shown for observed post-treatment biopsies, scGen, target-derived WOT, a CellRank terminal-lineage proxy, and the frozen Leca-VC prediction under responder and non-responder scenarios. Error bars denote 95% sample/biopsy bootstrap confidence intervals. CellRank does not natively generate Post expression; its dagger-marked composition is a post-hoc Pre-only proxy obtained by mapping four terminal lineages to broad cell types with the frozen Pre-only classifier and summing sample-level fate probabilities.
