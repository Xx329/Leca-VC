GSE230538 held-out cell-state composition benchmark. Observed Day 4 and Day 33 state proportions are compared with scGen, target-derived WOT, an untreated-only CellRank terminal-expression proxy, and Leca-VC. scGen, WOT, and CellRank compositions are post-hoc, expression-derived deterministic proxies obtained by expanding each frozen cell-line mean expression target over its matched untreated cells and applying the original marker/module state rule; they are not native single-cell composition outputs. Bars show means, and error bars show plus or minus one population SD across four cell lines for Observed/external proxies or three frozen simulation seeds for Leca-VC. Day 4 and Day 33 were held out from Leca-VC model generation.
