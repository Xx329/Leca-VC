Fig. S8. Assessment of benchmark-outcome retrieval from de-identified runtime prompts. (A) Response classification across 30 probe evaluations, comprising 10 frozen runtime contexts per dataset. Responses were scored against pre-specified held-out outcomes as correct, incorrect, or unknown, where “unknown” included refusals and responses without a scorable prediction. (B) Observed correct-response proportions. Points show the observed accuracy and whiskers denote two-sided 95% Wilson binomial confidence intervals (n = 10 per dataset and n = 30 pooled). Because of the small per-dataset sample size, an observed result of 0/10 has a 95% confidence interval of 0–27.8%. The pooled accuracy was 3.3% (1/30; 95% CI, 0.6–16.7%). Under this operational probe, the exact de-identified runtime prompts did not support reliable retrieval of benchmark-specific later outcomes. This experiment evaluates prompt-enabled outcome retrieval and does not exclude prior exposure of the language model to the underlying studies during pre-training.
